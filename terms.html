What is free

KNURL is free to download. The PIVOT machine, its five bench parts, and all five finishes — brass, steel, copper, black oxide and titanium — are free permanently, with no purchase and no time limit. A finish changes the feel and the sound of every machine, and cuts a new face into each one; it is free because a haptic is not something anyone can judge from a screenshot.

THE FULL DRAWER

Everything else — the other five machines (KNOB, SNAP, ZIPPER, LIGHTER, PLUNGER) and the wear they earn with use — is sold two ways. Either one opens all of it:

  • One-year unlock, bought once. It opens the drawer for 365 days and then simply ends. It is not a subscription: nothing renews, nothing bills again, and there is nothing to cancel. Buying another year afterwards is entirely up to you.
  • One-time purchase, bought once and kept permanently.

Prices are shown inside the app in your own currency, and are set per country by the App Store.

When a year ends

When a one-year unlock lapses, the five machines close again and the free bench stays exactly as it was. Anything the machines earned while the drawer was open — the wear, the odometer — is kept, and reappears if the drawer is opened again by another year or by the one-time unlock. No charge is ever made at the end of a year.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple and are subject to their policy. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves.

What a lifetime purchase means

The one-time unlock opens every machine in the drawer and every machine added to it afterwards. It will not be revoked, downgraded, or converted into a subscription.
