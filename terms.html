What is free

KNURL is free to download. The PIVOT machine, its five bench parts, and all five finishes — brass, steel, copper, black oxide and titanium — are free permanently, with no purchase and no time limit. A finish changes the feel and the sound of every machine; it is free because a haptic is not something anyone can judge from a screenshot.

THE FULL DRAWER

Everything else — the other six machines, wear-in across the whole bench, the finish rack and the plate faces — is sold two ways. Either one opens all of it:

  • Yearly subscription, beginning with a 7-day free trial. It renews automatically each year until cancelled.
  • One-time purchase, bought once and kept permanently.

Prices are shown inside the app in your own currency, and are set per country by the App Store.

The free trial

The trial runs 7 days and is available once per Apple ID. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, it converts to a paid year automatically at the price shown when you subscribed. If you buy the one-time unlock during a trial, the trial ends and nothing further is charged.

Renewal and cancellation

A subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the period ends. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the period ending. Manage or cancel any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device — the app also links there directly. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple and are subject to their policy. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves.

What a lifetime purchase means

The one-time unlock opens every machine in the drawer and every machine added to it afterwards. It will not be revoked, downgraded, or converted into a subscription.
